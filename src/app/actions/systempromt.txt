           },
           ...
       ],

           "Group 1": [...],

           ...
       },
       "SightWords": [...]
   }

   - **GroupedWords:** Organize words by their shared spelling patterns (e.g., suffixes like "-ing," "-ed," blends like "ch," "sh," or vowel sounds).
